Getting Started with MerchantBridge

Document ID: MBFS-FAQ-01 · Last updated: 1 June 2026

What does MerchantBridge do?

MerchantBridge helps UK businesses accept card payments in person (with our BridgePay terminals) and online (with hosted checkout, payment links, invoices and our API). We pay your card takings into your existing business bank account. Eligible merchants can also get funding through BridgeAdvance, our merchant cash advance.

Is MerchantBridge a bank? Can I open a business current account with you?

No. MerchantBridge is a payment institution, not a bank. We do not offer business or personal current accounts, savings, overdrafts, loans, credit cards or insurance. You need your own UK bank account to receive payouts.

Money waiting to be paid out to you is safeguarded in segregated accounts with our banking partners, as required for payment institutions. It is not covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).

Who can sign up?

UK-registered limited companies and LLPs, UK-resident sole traders and partnerships, and registered UK charities. You must be 18 or over and have a UK bank account in the business's name (or your own name if you are a sole trader). We can't accept businesses based outside the UK, including in the Republic of Ireland or elsewhere in the EEA. Some business types are prohibited or need extra checks — see our Prohibited and Restricted Businesses Policy.

What documents do I need?

For each person we need to verify (you, and any directors or owners of 25% or more):

  • a valid passport or UK photocard driving licence; and
  • proof of home address from the last 3 months — a utility bill, council tax bill, bank statement or HMRC letter. Mobile phone bills and online banking screenshots aren't accepted.

Limited companies also need their Companies House number.

How long does it take to get approved?

Most businesses are verified within 2 business days of sending us everything we need. Some checks take up to 10 business days.

Can I take payments before I'm approved?

Yes — you can accept up to £2,000 in card payments while we finish verifying you. We hold the money in your MerchantBridge balance and pay it out once you're verified. If your application isn't approved, we refund those payments to your customers and don't charge you any fees on them.

I'm switching from another provider. Can you tell me if you're cheaper?

We can't comment on other providers' pricing. The best way to compare is to take a recent statement from your current provider and work out what you'd pay with us using our published rates — our sales team (020 7946 0850) can help you with our side of the calculation. Remember to check your current provider's notice period and any early termination fees before you switch.

Can I get information in a different format?

Yes. We can provide documents in large print, Braille or audio. You can call us via Relay UK (dial 18001 then 020 7946 0800) or use a British Sign Language video relay service from your dashboard. If your circumstances make it harder to manage your account, let us know and we'll look at what extra support we can offer.

I'm changing from a sole trader to a limited company. Can I keep my account?

No — accounts can't be transferred between legal entities. Your new company will need to make a new application. Keep your old account open until the new one is verified so you can keep taking payments.
