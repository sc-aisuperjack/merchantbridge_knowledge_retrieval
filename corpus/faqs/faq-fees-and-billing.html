Business (commercial) cards, American Express, cards issued outside the UK and EEA, and phone/mail orders are charged differently — see the full Fee and Rate Schedule.

Do you charge more for foreign cards?

Yes. Cards issued outside the UK and EEA have an extra 1.50% added to the normal rate. What counts is where the card was issued, not the customer's nationality or the currency they pay in. American Express is a flat 2.75% in person and 2.95% + 20p online, wherever the card is from.

How do you take your fees?

Transaction fees are taken from your takings before we pay you. Monthly plan fees and terminal rental are taken from your balance on the 1st of the month. If there isn't enough in your balance, we collect by Direct Debit. If a Direct Debit fails, there's a £10 fee.

Do you charge VAT?

Card processing fees are exempt from VAT. Monthly plan fees, terminals, delivery, paper rolls, paper statements and early termination fees have VAT at 20% added. Your monthly VAT invoice is in your dashboard by the 5th business day of the month.

What's the Minimum Monthly Service Charge?

It only applies to the Scale plan. If you pay less than £150 in transaction fees in a month, we charge the difference. For example, if your fees for the month come to £120, we'll charge an extra £30.

Can I pass card fees on to my customers?

You can't add a surcharge when consumers pay with a personal debit or credit card — that's against UK law. You can surcharge business (commercial) cards, but no more than it costs you to accept them. You're free to offer a discount for other payment methods. If you're unsure, please take your own legal advice.

Is there a fee for chargebacks?

Yes, £15 per chargeback. On Growth and Scale we refund it if the dispute is decided in your favour. Pre-arbitration and arbitration cost £250 and aren't refundable.

Do I get my fee back if I refund a customer?

No. There's no charge for refunding, but we don't return the original transaction fee. If you void a payment before 22:00 on the same day, there's no fee at all.

What's the PCI non-compliance fee?

Every business that takes cards must confirm it's PCI DSS compliant by filling in a short questionnaire in the dashboard — within 90 days of opening your account and then every year. If you don't, we charge £35 a month until you do.

Can you put your prices up?

We'll always give you at least two months' notice of any increase. If you don't want to accept it, you can leave free of charge before it takes effect — even if you're in a minimum term.

Can I get paper statements?

Yes, for £2.50 + VAT each. Digital statements in the dashboard are free.

Can you give me Enterprise rates?

Enterprise pricing is tailored to each business and is only available from our sales team on 020 7946 0850 (Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00). Our support team can't quote Enterprise rates.
