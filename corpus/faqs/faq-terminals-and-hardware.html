My terminal is declining every card. What should I do?

If every card is declining, the problem is usually the connection or your account, not your customers' cards. Try these steps in order:

  1. Check the connection. Look for the 4G/Wi-Fi/Ethernet symbol. For the Mini, make sure Bluetooth is on and the MerchantBridge app is open on your phone.
  2. Restart the terminal. Hold the power button for 10 seconds, then switch it back on.
  3. Check for updates. A terminal that has missed updates for more than 30 days may stop taking payments. Leave it on charge and connected, then restart.
  4. Check your dashboard for any message about your account — for example, a verification request, the £2,000 pre-verification limit being reached, or a restriction on your account.
  5. Check our status page at status.merchantbridge.example for any service incidents.
  6. If it still isn't working, call support on 020 7946 0800. If it's a hardware fault, we'll send a free replacement by next-business-day delivery.

If only some cards are declining, the customer's bank is usually declining the payment. The customer should contact their bank or try another card. We're not told the reason for an issuer decline.

Can I take payments if my internet goes down?

The Go and Counter have an offline mode for up to 50 payments of up to £100 each. Reconnect within 24 hours so the payments can be sent. Offline payments are only approved when they're sent, so if a card is then declined, you won't receive that money. The Mini doesn't have an offline mode.

My terminal has been lost or stolen. What do I do?

Call our 24/7 Fraud & Emergency Line on 020 7946 0999 straight away so we can block the terminal. We'll send a replacement for £59 (Mini), £120 (Go) or £95 (Counter), plus VAT. If it's a rented terminal, your rental continues on the replacement.

My terminal is faulty. Will I have to pay?

Not if it's covered by warranty (12 months for the Mini; for as long as you rent a Go or Counter). Accidental and liquid damage aren't covered. Please send the faulty terminal back within 14 days of getting your replacement.

Where can I get paper rolls?

Order them in your dashboard: £12 + VAT for 20 rolls (57 mm × 40 mm thermal). Your first Go or Counter order comes with 20 free rolls.

What's the contactless limit?

We don't set one. The limit is set by the customer's card issuer — most UK cards have a £100 limit. Above that, or from time to time for security, the customer will be asked to insert their card and enter their PIN.

How do I return a rented terminal?

When your rental ends we'll email you a prepaid returns label. Please send the terminal, charger and base back within 30 days. If we don't receive it, we'll charge £150 + VAT for a Go or £120 + VAT for a Counter. If you end a rental within the first 12 months, an early termination fee of 50% of the remaining rental applies.

Someone has turned up saying they need to service my terminal. Is that okay?

No. We never send engineers unannounced — replacements always come by courier after you've contacted us. Don't hand over your terminal, and call 020 7946 0999 to report it.
