Payments and Payouts

Document ID: MBFS-FAQ-02 · Last updated: 14 March 2026

Synthetic document. MerchantBridge Financial Services Ltd is a fictional company created for testing and training purposes. All names, figures, phone numbers and addresses are invented.

When will I get paid?

It depends on your plan:

PlanWhen we pay you
Starter2 business days after the day your sales are processed
Growth and ScaleThe next business day after your sales are processed

Sales taken before 22:00 on a business day are processed that day. Sales after 22:00, or at weekends and on bank holidays, are processed on the next business day. We send payouts by Faster Payments at 09:00 and they normally arrive within a couple of hours.

I took payments on Saturday. When will I see them?

Weekend sales are processed on Monday. On Growth or Scale you'll be paid on Tuesday; on Starter, on Wednesday. If Monday is a bank holiday, everything moves back a day.

What happens over bank holidays?

We don't process batches or send standard payouts on bank holidays in England and Wales. Before Christmas, New Year and Easter we publish a payout calendar as a Service Notice showing exactly when each day's sales will be paid.

Why is my payout less than my sales?

We pay you your sales minus transaction fees, refunds, any chargebacks and, if one applies to your account, a reserve. Monthly fees are also taken from your balance on the 1st of each month. Your dashboard shows a full breakdown for every payout.

Can I get my money faster?

Yes, with Instant Payouts. You can send your available balance to your bank account in around 30 minutes, any time of day, any day of the week. Instant Payouts cost 1.0% of the amount (minimum £0.50), up to £10,000 per payout and 5 payouts a day.

Instant Payouts are available on the Growth and Scale plans only. Your account must have been verified and open for at least 30 days.

My payout hasn't arrived. What should I do?

  1. Check the payout status and your bank details in the dashboard.
  2. Check whether we've emailed you about a hold or reserve.
  3. If the payout day has ended and it still hasn't arrived, contact us with the payout reference and we'll trace it within 2 business days.

How do I change my bank account?

Go to Settings › Payouts in the dashboard. You'll need to confirm the change with two-factor authentication. For your security, the first payout to a new account is delayed by 72 hours. We'll never ask you to change bank details over the phone or by email.

Can I be paid in euros or dollars?

On Growth and Scale you can be paid in GBP, EUR or USD. You'll need a bank account in that currency in your business's name. If we convert currency for you, we use the mid-market rate with a markup (1.25% on Growth, 0.90% on Scale). Starter merchants are paid in GBP only.

How do I refund a customer?

From the dashboard, your terminal (using a manager PIN) or the API. Refunds go back to the card that was used to pay and usually reach the customer in 3–10 business days. There's no fee for refunding, but the original transaction fee isn't returned. If you spot a mistake on the same day, void the payment before 22:00 instead — there's no fee at all for a void.

Can I give a customer a cash refund for a card payment?

No. Card payments must be refunded to the original card. Cash refunds for card payments are against card scheme rules.

Is there a minimum payout?

Yes, £1. Anything below this is carried forward to your next payout.
