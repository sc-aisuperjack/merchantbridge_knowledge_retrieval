Disputes, Security and Your Account

Document ID: MBFS-FAQ-05 · Last updated: 20 July 2026

Chargebacks

I've received a chargeback. What happens now?

We'll have emailed you and added the chargeback to your dashboard. The disputed amount and the chargeback fee have been taken from your balance. You have 7 calendar days to accept it or challenge it with evidence. If you don't respond, it's accepted automatically.

The customer who raised a chargeback is now asking me for a refund. Should I refund them?

No. The money has already been taken from your balance for the chargeback. If you refund as well, your customer gets paid twice and you can't get the refund back. If you agree the customer should have their money back, simply accept the chargeback in your dashboard. If you'd already refunded them before the chargeback arrived, challenge it and upload proof of the refund.

How long does a chargeback take?

Usually 30–75 days after you send us your evidence, and occasionally up to 120 days. The decision is made by the customer's bank, not by MerchantBridge.

Security

Someone called saying they're from MerchantBridge and asked for a code. Is it really you?

We will never ask for your password, a two-factor code, your secret API key, or ask you to change your bank details, move money or install software. If someone does, hang up — it's a scam. Call our 24/7 Fraud & Emergency Line on 020 7946 0999 (dial it yourself; don't use a number the caller gave you). If you've already shared a code, call us immediately so we can freeze payouts, refunds and bank detail changes, and then change your password.

Do I have to use two-factor authentication?

Yes. It's mandatory for everyone who logs in to the dashboard. We recommend an authenticator app rather than SMS.

I'm seeing lots of small failed payments on my website. What's going on?

This could be a card testing attack, where fraudsters check stolen card numbers. Call 020 7946 0999 and we can switch on extra protection straight away.

Holds and reserves

Why are my funds being held?

We sometimes hold funds to protect against refunds, chargebacks or fraud — for example, if your chargebacks increase, you take payments far in advance, or there's unusual activity. We'll tell you within 1 business day what kind of hold it is and how long it should last. Occasionally the law prevents us from telling you the reason.

Your account

How do I close my account?

The account Owner can close it in Settings › Account › Close account, or by emailing support from the Owner's registered email address. Starter accounts close straight away; Growth needs 30 days' notice; Scale has a 12-month initial term with an early termination fee if you leave early. Rented terminals must be returned within 30 days. We may hold some of your balance for up to 120 days after closure to cover late refunds and chargebacks.

Can you delete my data when I close my account?

We'll delete what we no longer need, but the law requires us to keep some records — for example, identity checks for 5 years after you leave and transaction records for 6 years. Send requests to dpo@merchantbridge.example and we'll respond within one month.

How do I make a complaint?

Call 020 7946 0800, email complaints@merchantbridge.example, or use live chat. We'll acknowledge it within 2 business days. For most complaints about payments we'll send our final response within 15 business days. If you're not happy with our answer, you may be able to take your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

Will you compensate me for lost sales if your service goes down?

Our terms don't cover indirect losses such as lost sales or profits, except where the law says otherwise. If we've made a mistake — for example, charging you incorrectly or paying you late — we'll put it right. You can always raise a complaint and we'll look at your circumstances individually.
