1. WHICH DOCUMENT WINS WHEN DOCUMENTS DISAGREE
======================================================================

The MerchantBridge knowledge base contains four types of document. If two
documents give different information, apply this order of precedence
(1 = highest):

  1. SERVICE NOTICES (MBFS-NTC-01 onwards) - from their stated effective
     date only. Before the effective date, the earlier position applies.
  2. FEE AND RATE SCHEDULE (MBFS-DATA-01, fee-and-rate-schedule.csv) -
     for any fee, rate, price or limit. Always check the effective_from
     and effective_to columns against today's date.
  3. POLICIES (MBFS-POL-01 to MBFS-POL-15).
  4. FAQ PAGES (MBFS-FAQ-01 to MBFS-FAQ-05). FAQs are simplified summaries
     and may be updated less often than policies. Each FAQ page shows a
     "Last updated" date - if it is older than a relevant notice or policy,
     the notice or policy applies.

When an answer depends on a date (for example, a fee that is changing),
give the position that applies today AND mention the upcoming change and
its effective date.

When a document says a fee or rule "applies at the effective date of this
policy", check the schedule and notices for later changes.

2. WHAT THE KNOWLEDGE BASE DOES NOT COVER
======================================================================

MerchantBridge provides merchant card acquiring, payment terminals, online
payments, payouts and the BridgeAdvance merchant cash advance. It does NOT
provide, and support must not give information or advice about:

  - business or personal current accounts, savings accounts, overdrafts,
    loans, mortgages, credit cards or insurance;
  - tax, accounting, legal or investment advice;
  - pricing or features of other payment providers, or comparisons with them;
  - Enterprise pricing (handled by the sales team only);
  - the reasons for a payout hold where the law prevents disclosure.

For these, explain politely that it is outside what MerchantBridge can help
with, and where appropriate suggest the merchant seeks independent
professional advice or contacts the relevant team.

4. GLOSSARY
======================================================================

ARN (Acquirer Reference Number)
    A reference that lets a card issuer trace a refund or payment.
Batch cut-off
    22:00 UK time on a business day. Transactions after this time, or on a
    non-business day, go into the next business day's batch.
Business day
    Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England and Wales.
Card-not-present (CNP) / Online
    Payments where the card is not physically presented: hosted checkout,
    payment links, invoices, API, plugins and MOTO.
Chargeback
    A reversal of a card payment initiated by the cardholder's bank.
Chargeback ratio
    Chargebacks received in a calendar month divided by card transactions
    processed in that month.
Closing reserve
    Funds held after an account closes to cover late refunds and chargebacks.
DCC (Dynamic Currency Conversion)
    Letting a foreign cardholder pay in their home currency at the terminal.
    MerchantBridge does NOT offer DCC.
Factor rate
    The multiplier that sets the total repayable on a BridgeAdvance.
Holdback
    The percentage of daily card settlements used to repay a BridgeAdvance.
Instant Payout
    An on-demand payout of available balance, for a fee.
International card
    A card issued outside the UK and EEA. Determined by the card's issuing
    country, not by the currency of the payment or the customer's nationality.
KYB
    Know Your Business - verification of a business and its owners.
MMSC
    Minimum Monthly Service Charge (Scale plan only).
MOTO
    Mail order / telephone order - card details keyed into the virtual terminal.
Net settlement
    Gross sales minus fees, refunds, chargebacks, reserves and other deductions.
PCI DSS
    Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard.
PSC
    Person with significant control - owns or controls 25% or more.
Rolling reserve
    A percentage of each day's settlement held for a set period.
SCA / 3D Secure
    Strong Customer Authentication for online card payments.
T+1 / T+2
    Payout one / two business days after the processing day.
Void
    Cancelling a payment before the batch cut-off; no fee is charged.
