----------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

From 1 September 2026, merchants on the Starter plan can use Instant
Payouts to send their available balance to their bank account at any time,
instead of waiting for their standard T+2 payout.

This is a new optional service. Standard payouts remain free and are not
changing. Nothing changes for Growth or Scale merchants.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
TERMS FOR STARTER MERCHANTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Fee .......................... 1.5% of the payout amount,
                                 minimum GBP 0.50 per Instant Payout
                                 (VAT-exempt)
  Maximum per Instant Payout ... GBP 2,500
  Maximum per day .............. 3 Instant Payouts
  Currency ..................... GBP only
  Eligibility .................. account verified and open for at least
                                 30 days; no active payout hold or reserve
                                 review
  Speed ........................ usually within 30 minutes, 24/7

For comparison, Growth and Scale merchants continue to pay 1.0%
(minimum GBP 0.50), with a limit of GBP 10,000 per Instant Payout and 5 per day.

Worked example (Starter): an Instant Payout of GBP 400 costs
GBP 400 x 1.5% = GBP 6.00, so GBP 394.00 is sent to your bank account.
An Instant Payout of GBP 20 costs the minimum GBP 0.50 (1.5% would be
GBP 0.30), so GBP 19.50 is sent.
