----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS CHANGING
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The fee we charge for each chargeback will increase:

  Up to and including 31 December 2026 ..... GBP 15 per chargeback
  From 1 January 2027 ...................... GBP 20 per chargeback

The fee that applies is determined by the date we NOTIFY you of the
chargeback, not the date of the original transaction. A chargeback notified
on 31 December 2026 is charged GBP 15; a chargeback notified on 2 January
2027 is charged GBP 20, even if the original payment was made in 2026.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS NOT CHANGING
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - On Growth and Scale, the chargeback fee is still refunded if the dispute
    is decided in your favour. On Starter, it remains non-refundable.
  - The pre-arbitration / arbitration fee stays at GBP 250 (non-refundable).
  - Retrieval request fees stay at GBP 5 (Starter and Growth) and GBP 0 (Scale).
  - Your 7-calendar-day deadline to respond to a chargeback is unchanged.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Card schemes have increased the fees they charge us for handling disputes,
and our dispute-handling costs have risen. We have not increased this fee
since 2023.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR RIGHTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

We are giving you more than two months' notice of this increase. If you do
not wish to accept it, you may close your account free of charge -
including any early termination fee for the Scale plan or for terminal
rentals - at any time before 1 January 2027. To do so, contact support or
use Settings > Account > Close account and mention this notice.

If we do not hear from you, we will treat you as having accepted the change.

The Fee and Rate Schedule has been updated (rows DSP-001 to DSP-004).
Our Fees and Billing FAQ and Disputes FAQ will be updated on the effective date.
