# Complaints Handling Policy

## 1. What counts as a complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, oral or written, about our products or services, whether justified or not. You do not need to use the word "complaint". Support agents must record a complaint whenever a merchant is unhappy with something we have done or failed to do, and must not discourage anyone from complaining.

Please tell us your merchant ID, what went wrong, and what you would like us to do to put it right.

## 3. Our timescales

| Step | Timescale |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within **2 business days** |
| Final response — complaints about **payment services** (card processing, payouts, refunds, chargebacks, terminals used to take payments) | Within **15 business days** of receipt. In exceptional circumstances we will send a holding response explaining the delay, and a final response within **35 business days** |
| Final response — **other complaints** (including **BridgeAdvance**) | Within **8 weeks** of receipt |

If we resolve your complaint to your satisfaction by the end of the third business day after receipt, we will confirm this in writing ("summary resolution communication").

## 4. Putting things right

Where we have made a mistake we will put you back in the position you would have been in, where reasonably possible — for example, by refunding fees charged in error or paying interest on late payouts at the Bank of England base rate. We may also make a **goodwill payment** for distress or inconvenience.

Under our terms and conditions we are not liable for **indirect or consequential losses**, such as loss of profit or lost sales, except where the law says otherwise. Each complaint is still considered on its individual merits.

## 5. Financial Ombudsman Service

If you are unhappy with our final response, or we have not sent one within the timescales above, you may be able to refer your complaint to the **Financial Ombudsman Service** (FOS), a free and independent service.

- The FOS can usually consider complaints from **micro-enterprises** (fewer than 10 employees and annual turnover or balance sheet of no more than **€2 million**), **small businesses** (fewer than 50 employees and annual turnover below **£6.5 million**, or a balance sheet below **£5 million**), and charities with annual income below **£6.5 million**.
- You must refer your complaint within **6 months** of our final response.
- Complaints about BridgeAdvance may fall outside the FOS's jurisdiction because merchant cash advances are not regulated credit. We will tell you in our final response whether you can refer your complaint.
- Our final response will include the FOS's contact details.

## 6. Customers in vulnerable circumstances

Anyone can find themselves in vulnerable circumstances — for example, through bereavement, illness, financial difficulty, or a sudden change in their business. If you tell us about your circumstances we will:

- record them (with your consent) so you do not have to repeat yourself;
- offer extra time, a named contact, or communication in a different format;
- consider pausing collection of debts or BridgeAdvance repayments while we discuss options.

## 7. Accessibility

We can provide documents in **large print, Braille or audio**. You can contact us using **Relay UK** (prefix 18001 before our number) or via a **British Sign Language video relay service** from the dashboard.

## 8. Record keeping

We keep complaint records for at least **5 years** from the date of the final response.
