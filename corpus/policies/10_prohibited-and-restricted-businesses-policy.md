# Prohibited and Restricted Businesses Policy

## 1. Purpose

Card schemes, our banking partners and the law limit the types of business we can support. This policy lists businesses we **cannot** support (prohibited) and businesses we can support only **with additional checks or conditions** (restricted).

## 2. Prohibited businesses

We cannot provide services to businesses that sell or are involved in:

- weapons, firearms, ammunition, explosives and weapon parts, including 3D-printed weapon files;
- illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia, and products marketed as "legal highs";
- adult content and services, including pornography, escort services and webcam services;
- unlicensed gambling, and all gambling not licensed by the UK Gambling Commission;
- cryptocurrency exchanges, crypto wallets, crypto ATMs, and the sale of NFTs;
- money transfer, currency exchange (bureaux de change) and other money services businesses;
- payday lending, debt collection and credit repair services;
- multi-level marketing, pyramid and "get rich quick" schemes;
- timeshares and holiday clubs;
- counterfeit or replica goods, or goods that infringe intellectual property;
- prescription-only medicines (unless a registered pharmacy — see restricted list);
- any product or service that is illegal in the UK.

## 3. Restricted businesses

We can support these businesses only if they meet the conditions shown. Approval is **at MerchantBridge's discretion** and is not guaranteed even if the conditions are met. Additional verification may extend onboarding to 10 business days (MBFS-POL-01) and we may apply a reserve (MBFS-POL-11).

| Business type | Conditions |
|---|---|
| **CBD products** | No smokable or vapeable CBD products; products must not contain controlled cannabinoids above legal limits (independent **certificate of analysis** required for each product line, showing THC no more than **1 mg per container**); ingestible products must be listed on the Food Standards Agency public list; **no medical claims** |
| **E-cigarettes, vapes and tobacco** | Robust **age verification** at checkout (online) and at point of sale; compliance with UK product notification rules; no disposable vapes; no sales to under-18s |
| **Alcohol** | Valid premises licence; age verification for online and delivery sales |
| **Licensed gambling** | Operating licence from the UK Gambling Commission; Scale or Enterprise plan only |
| **Travel agents and tour operators** | ATOL licence or other financial protection for package holidays; reserve normally applies because customers pay in advance |
| **Events and ticketing** | Evidence of event history; reserve may apply for events more than 30 days away |
| **Pre-orders and advance payments (more than 30 days)** | Delivery timeline evidence; reserve normally applies |
| **Dating and introduction services** | Clear cancellation terms; no adult content |
| **Registered pharmacies** | GPhC registration; prescription verification for prescription-only medicines |
| **Nutritional supplements and weight-loss products** | No unauthorised health claims; no free-trial or negative-option billing |
| **Charities and fundraising** | Registered charity number (see MBFS-POL-01) |

## 4. Changes to what you sell

You must tell us at least **14 days before** you start selling a new category of goods or services. If you start selling prohibited goods or services, or restricted goods without approval, we may hold payouts, refuse transactions or close your account (MBFS-POL-11 and MBFS-POL-12).

## 5. How support agents should respond

Agents must not confirm that a restricted business will be approved. They should explain the conditions, explain that a risk review will take place, and invite the merchant to apply.
