# Rolling Reserves and Risk Holds Policy

## 1. Why we hold funds

When you take a card payment, MerchantBridge remains liable to the card schemes for refunds and chargebacks on that payment for many months. If a merchant cannot cover these, we must pay them. To manage this risk we may hold back some or all of your funds using the measures below.

## 2. Types of risk measure

| Measure | How it works | Typical parameters |
|---|---|---|
| **Rolling reserve** | A percentage of each day's settlement is held and released automatically after a set period | **5%–15%** held for **90–180 days** |
| **Fixed reserve** | A fixed sum is held for as long as the reserve applies | Usually equal to 1–3 months of expected refunds and chargebacks |
| **Extended payout schedule** | Your payout schedule is lengthened | Up to **7 business days** after the processing day |
| **Payout hold** | Payouts are paused while we investigate | Normally up to **10 business days** |
| **Closing reserve** | Funds are held after an account closes | Up to **120 days**, or up to **180 days** for advance-payment businesses |

Example of a rolling reserve: with a 10% rolling reserve held for 90 days, if your settlement on 1 March is £1,000, we pay out £900 on your normal payout day and release the remaining £100 on 30 May.

## 3. When we may apply a reserve or hold

Including, but not limited to:

- your **chargeback ratio** exceeds **0.90%** in a calendar month (see MBFS-POL-04);
- your business takes **payment in advance** (travel, events, pre-orders more than 30 days ahead);
- your business type is **restricted** (MBFS-POL-10);
- a sudden, unexplained increase in volume, average transaction value or refunds;
- signs of fraud, account takeover or card testing;
- you change what you sell without telling us;
- negative balances that are not repaid;
- we are required to by law, a regulator, a court or a card scheme.

## 4. Notification

- We will tell you by email and in your dashboard within **1 business day** of applying a reserve or hold, and explain what it is and how long it is expected to last.
- In some cases the law prevents us from telling you **why** we are holding funds (for example, where we have made a report to a law enforcement agency). In those cases we can only tell you that a hold is in place. Support agents must not speculate about the reason.

## 5. Review and release

- Rolling and fixed reserves are reviewed at least every **90 days**. You can ask for an earlier review by providing evidence (for example, a reduced chargeback ratio for two consecutive months, or delivery of pre-ordered goods).
- Payout holds are released as soon as our investigation is complete. If we need information from you, the hold continues until you provide it.
- Holds may be extended beyond 10 business days where we are legally required to do so.

## 6. Closing reserve

When an account closes (for whatever reason), we may hold the remaining balance as a closing reserve to cover refunds and chargebacks that arrive after closure:

- normally up to **120 days** after closure;
- up to **180 days** for businesses that took advance payments.

Any remaining balance is paid to your nominated bank account at the end of the reserve period, together with a final statement.

## 7. Disputes about reserves

If you disagree with a reserve or hold, you can ask for a review through your account manager or support, or make a complaint under MBFS-POL-13.
