# BridgeAdvance Merchant Cash Advance Policy

## 1. What BridgeAdvance is

BridgeAdvance is a **merchant cash advance**. We pay you a lump sum now in exchange for an agreed share of your **future card sales** processed through MerchantBridge. You repay automatically as a fixed percentage of each day's card settlements.

**BridgeAdvance is not a loan.** It has:

- **no interest** and **no APR** — instead there is a single **fixed fee**, agreed upfront;
- **no fixed repayment term** — you repay faster when sales are high and slower when sales are low;
- **no fixed monthly payment**.

Merchant cash advances are **not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority** as consumer credit. BridgeAdvance is only available to businesses, not for personal use.

## 2. Eligibility

To be offered BridgeAdvance you must:

- be on the **Growth or Scale** plan (Starter merchants are not eligible);
- have been **trading for at least 12 months**;
- have processed card payments with MerchantBridge for at least **3 months**;
- have **average monthly card turnover of at least £5,000** through MerchantBridge over the last 3 months;
- have no active rolling reserve, payout hold, or chargeback ratio above 0.90%;
- have no other BridgeAdvance outstanding (see section 6 for renewals).

Offers are made at MerchantBridge's discretion. Eligible merchants see an offer in their dashboard; you cannot apply for an amount that has not been offered. We carry out a soft search with a credit reference agency on the business and its directors, which does not affect credit scores.

## 3. How much

- From **£2,000** to **£250,000**.
- No more than **100%** of your average monthly card turnover through MerchantBridge over the last 3 months.

## 4. Cost: the fixed fee

The fee is expressed as a **factor rate** between **1.08** and **1.25**, set by MerchantBridge based on your sales history and risk. The total you repay is:

> **Total repayable = advance amount × factor rate**
> **Fixed fee = total repayable − advance amount**

*Example:* a £10,000 advance with a factor rate of 1.15 means a total repayable of **£11,500** and a fixed fee of **£1,500**.

The fixed fee does not increase if repayment takes longer, and does **not decrease** if you repay faster or settle early. The fee is outside the scope of VAT.

## 5. Repayment

- A fixed **holdback percentage**, between **10% and 20%**, is deducted from each day's card settlements before payout until the total repayable has been collected.
- *Example:* with a 12% holdback and £500 of card sales settled in a day, £60 goes towards the advance and the remainder (less transaction fees) is paid out.
- Card sales processed elsewhere are not counted. Routing card sales away from MerchantBridge to avoid repayment is a breach of the agreement, and the full outstanding balance becomes due immediately.
- You can **settle early** at any time by paying the remaining balance by bank transfer. There is no discount on the fixed fee.
- If no repayment is received for **60 days** (for example because you have stopped trading), we will contact you to agree a plan. We may then collect a minimum of **£5 per business day** by Direct Debit, or ask for the outstanding balance.
- If your account closes, any outstanding BridgeAdvance balance becomes due immediately (MBFS-POL-12).

## 6. Renewals

Once you have repaid at least **60%** of the total repayable, you may be offered a renewal. The remaining balance of your current advance is deducted from the new advance amount.

## 7. Financial difficulty

If you are struggling with repayments, contact us as soon as possible. We may be able to pause or reduce collections. See also MBFS-POL-13 (customers in vulnerable circumstances).

## 8. What support agents can and cannot say

Agents can explain how BridgeAdvance works, eligibility, and illustrative calculations using figures the merchant provides. Agents **cannot** promise an offer, quote an individual factor rate before an offer is made, compare BridgeAdvance with loans or other providers' products, or advise whether a merchant should take an advance. Merchants should consider independent financial advice.

## 9. Complaints

Complaints about BridgeAdvance receive a final response within **8 weeks** (MBFS-POL-13). They may fall outside the Financial Ombudsman Service's jurisdiction.
