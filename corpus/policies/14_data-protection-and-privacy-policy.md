# Data Protection and Privacy Policy

## 1. Who we are

MerchantBridge Financial Services Ltd is the **controller** of the personal data described in this policy. Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) can be contacted at **dpo@merchantbridge.example** or by post at 1 Example Wharf, London SE1 0XX.

We process personal data in accordance with the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**.

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Identity and verification | Names, dates of birth, home addresses, ID documents and proof of address for applicants, directors, PSCs, partners and trustees |
| Business information | Company details, bank account, turnover, products sold |
| Transaction data | Card payments, refunds, chargebacks, payouts — including limited cardholder data (masked card number, card type, issuing country) |
| Usage and device data | Dashboard logins, IP addresses, device identifiers, terminal location (Go terminals only, when processing a payment) |
| Communications | Emails, chat transcripts and **call recordings** |

## 3. Why we use it and our lawful bases

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Verifying your identity and preventing financial crime | Legal obligation; legitimate interests |
| Providing payment services and support | Performance of a contract |
| Fraud prevention and risk management | Legitimate interests; legal obligation |
| Meeting card scheme rules and regulatory reporting | Legal obligation; legitimate interests |
| Improving our services and training staff | Legitimate interests |
| Marketing about our own products | Consent (for sole traders and partnerships) or legitimate interests (for companies). You can opt out at any time |

## 4. Who we share data with

Card schemes and issuers; our banking partners; fraud prevention agencies; credit reference agencies (for BridgeAdvance and onboarding checks); Companies House; regulators, law enforcement and courts where required; and service providers acting on our instructions (for example, cloud hosting, identity verification and call recording). We do **not sell** personal data.

Some service providers are located outside the UK. Where data is transferred outside the UK, we use adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Identity and verification records (KYB/KYC) | **5 years after the end of our business relationship** (required by the Money Laundering Regulations 2017) |
| Transaction records | **6 years** after the end of the financial year in which the transaction took place |
| Call recordings and chat transcripts | **2 years**, or longer if linked to a complaint or dispute |
| Complaint records | 5 years from the final response |
| Marketing preferences | Until you withdraw consent or 2 years after closure, whichever is sooner |
| Unsuccessful applications | 12 months from the decision (5 years where declined for financial-crime reasons) |

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** your personal data (a "subject access request");
- **rectification** of inaccurate data;
- **erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — this right does **not** apply where we must keep data to comply with a legal obligation, such as the anti-money-laundering and transaction record retention periods above;
- **restrict** or **object** to certain processing, including direct marketing (which we will always stop on request);
- **data portability** for data you provided to us under a contract.

We respond to rights requests within **one month**. For complex or multiple requests we may extend this by up to **two further months** and will tell you why within the first month. Requests are normally free of charge. We will need to verify your identity before responding.

**Closing your account does not automatically delete your data.** If you ask us to delete your data after closing your account, we will delete or anonymise data we no longer need (for example, marketing data) and explain which records we must keep and for how long.

## 7. How to make a request

Email **dpo@merchantbridge.example** or use the privacy request form in the dashboard. Support agents cannot fulfil rights requests themselves; they must pass them to the DPO team the same business day.

## 8. Cardholder data

Merchants are controllers of their own customers' data. MerchantBridge processes cardholder data to provide payment services and comply with card scheme rules. Cardholders asking about their data should contact the merchant first; requests about MerchantBridge's own processing can be sent to our DPO.

## 9. Complaints to the regulator

You have the right to complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.
