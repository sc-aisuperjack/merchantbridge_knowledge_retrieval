# Pricing Plans and Billing Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes our pricing plans, how transaction fees are calculated and collected, how VAT applies, and how we notify you of price changes. The **authoritative list of every fee and rate** is the *Fee and Rate Schedule* (`fee-and-rate-schedule.csv`, MBFS-DATA-01). Where this policy and the schedule differ on a figure, the schedule applies.

## 3. Transaction fees — structure

Transaction fees depend on your **plan**, the **channel** and the **type and origin of the card**:

- **Channel.** *In-person* (card present: chip, contactless or swipe on a BridgePay terminal) or *Online / card-not-present* (hosted checkout, payment links, invoices, API and e-commerce plugins). Online rates include a fixed per-transaction fee.
- **Card type.** Consumer debit, consumer credit, or commercial (business, corporate and purchasing) cards. Visa, Mastercard and Maestro cards are priced by card type. **American Express** has a single flat rate on all plans.
- **Card origin.** Rates in the schedule apply to cards issued in the **UK or EEA**. Cards issued **outside the UK and EEA** attract an **international card surcharge of 1.50%**, added to the applicable rate. The surcharge does not apply to American Express, whose flat rate already covers international cards.
- **MOTO.** Mail order / telephone order transactions keyed manually into the dashboard virtual terminal are charged at the applicable online rate **plus 0.50%**.

Example: on the Growth plan, an online payment with a UK consumer credit card is charged 1.89% + 20p. An in-person payment with a US-issued consumer credit card on the Scale plan is charged 1.49% + 1.50% = 2.99%.

## 4. How fees are calculated

- Percentage fees are applied to the **gross transaction amount**, including any tip or gratuity.
- Fees are calculated **per transaction** and rounded to the **nearest penny** (half a penny rounds up).
- Fees on transactions in a currency other than GBP are calculated in the transaction currency and converted to your settlement currency (see MBFS-POL-08).
- If a payment is **voided** (cancelled before the daily batch cut-off of 22:00 UK time) no fee is charged.
- If a payment is **refunded**, we do not charge a refund fee, but the original transaction fee is **not returned** (see MBFS-POL-05).

## 5. How fees are collected

- **Transaction fees** are deducted from your settlements before each payout ("net settlement"). Your payout will therefore be lower than your gross sales.
- **Monthly plan fees, terminal rental and other monthly charges** are billed on the **1st of each month** in advance and deducted from your balance.
- If your balance is insufficient, we collect the amount by **Direct Debit** from your nominated bank account. A **failed Direct Debit fee of £10** applies each time a Direct Debit is returned unpaid.
- Plan fees for part-months (for example, when you upgrade) are pro-rated by day.

## 6. VAT

| Charge | VAT treatment |
|---|---|
| Transaction processing fees, international surcharge, MOTO uplift, chargeback and dispute fees, Instant Payout fees, FX markup | Exempt from VAT |
| Monthly plan fees, terminal purchase and rental, replacement and unreturned-terminal charges, delivery, paper rolls, paper statements, early termination fees | Standard-rated (20%) |
| BridgeAdvance fixed fee | Outside the scope of VAT |

Prices for standard-rated items are quoted **excluding VAT** unless stated otherwise.

## 7. Statements and invoices

- A monthly statement and VAT invoice are available in your dashboard by the **5th business day** of each month.
- Paper statements can be posted on request for **£2.50 + VAT per statement**.

## 8. Minimum Monthly Service Charge (Scale only)

On the Scale plan, if the total transaction processing fees you pay in a calendar month are less than **£150**, we charge the shortfall as a Minimum Monthly Service Charge (MMSC). The MMSC is in addition to the monthly plan fee and is VAT-exempt. Example: if you pay £110 in processing fees in March, the MMSC for March is £40.

## 9. Changing plans

- **Upgrades** (Starter → Growth, Growth → Scale) take effect from the next day. The new monthly fee is pro-rated for the rest of the month.
- **Downgrades** take effect from the start of the next billing month.
- Downgrading from Scale during the 12-month initial term is treated as early termination of the Scale term and the early termination fee in MBFS-POL-12 applies.

## 10. Price changes

- We will give you at least **two months' notice** by email and in your dashboard before increasing any fee or introducing a new fee. Notices are also published as dated *Service Notices*.
- You may terminate your agreement **free of charge**, including any early termination fee, at any time before the change takes effect.
- Reductions in fees may take effect immediately.

## 11. Surcharging and discounts

- UK law **prohibits** charging consumers a surcharge for paying with a **consumer** debit or credit card. You must not add a fee to a customer's bill because they pay by consumer card.
- You may surcharge **commercial (business) cards**, but the surcharge must not exceed the **cost to you** of accepting that card.
- You may offer a **discount** for paying by another method, provided the discount is applied clearly and consistently.
- MerchantBridge cannot give legal advice on surcharging; merchants should take their own advice if unsure.
