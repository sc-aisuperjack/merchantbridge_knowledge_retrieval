# Account Opening and Business Verification Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains who can open a MerchantBridge merchant account, what information we need, how long verification takes and what happens while your account is being checked. We are required by the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017 and by card scheme rules to verify every business we work with ("Know Your Business", or KYB).

## 2. Who can apply

You can apply for a MerchantBridge account if **all** of the following apply:

1. Your business is one of:
   - a private or public limited company registered at Companies House in England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland;
   - a limited liability partnership (LLP) registered at Companies House;
   - a sole trader who is resident in the United Kingdom;
   - a general partnership whose partners are resident in the United Kingdom;
   - a charity registered with the Charity Commission, OSCR or the Charity Commission for Northern Ireland.
2. You have a **UK bank account** held in the name of the business (or, for sole traders, in your own name) that can receive Faster Payments in pounds sterling.
3. The person applying is **18 or over** and is authorised to act for the business.
4. Your business activity is not prohibited under the *Prohibited and Restricted Businesses Policy* (MBFS-POL-10).

## 3. Who cannot apply

We cannot open accounts for:

- businesses incorporated or resident **outside the United Kingdom**, including businesses in the Republic of Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man and the EEA — even if they sell to UK customers;
- sole traders or partners who are not UK resident;
- individuals who are not trading as a business (for example, selling personal belongings or collecting money for a private event);
- businesses that cannot provide a UK sterling bank account;
- businesses whose activity is prohibited (see MBFS-POL-10).

We do not make exceptions to the UK residency and UK bank account requirements. Customers should not be told that an exception may be possible.

## 4. Information and documents we ask for

| Business type | What we need |
|---|---|
| All applicants | Legal name, trading name, trading address, website or social media pages (if any), description of goods/services, expected monthly card turnover, average transaction value, and UK bank account details |
| Limited companies and LLPs | Companies House registration number; details of all directors (or designated members); details of every **person with significant control (PSC)** — anyone who owns or controls **25% or more** of the shares or voting rights |
| Sole traders | Full name, date of birth, home address and, if different, trading address |
| Partnerships | Details of every partner and a copy of the partnership agreement if one exists |
| Charities | Charity registration number and details of at least two trustees |

For each individual we need to verify (applicant, directors, PSCs, partners or trustees) we ask for:

- **photo ID**: a valid passport (any nationality) or a full or provisional UK photocard driving licence; and
- **proof of home address** dated within the **last 3 months**: a utility bill, council tax bill, bank or building society statement, or HMRC letter. Mobile phone bills and screenshots of online banking are not accepted.

We may also ask for proof of the bank account (a bank statement header or letter showing the account name, sort code and account number), copies of relevant licences (see MBFS-POL-10), or evidence of how your business is funded.

## 5. Verification timescales

- Most applications are verified within **2 business days** of us receiving complete information.
- Where we need to carry out additional checks (for example, for restricted business types, complex ownership structures, or where documents are unclear) verification can take up to **10 business days**.
- Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England and Wales.
- We will tell you by email and in your dashboard if we need anything else. The verification clock restarts when we receive the requested information.

## 6. Taking payments before verification is complete

To help you get started, once you have submitted your application you can begin accepting card payments **before** verification is complete, subject to the following limits:

- you can accept up to **£2,000 in total** in card payments before verification is complete;
- **no payouts** are made to your bank account until verification is complete — funds are held in your MerchantBridge balance;
- Instant Payouts and BridgeAdvance are not available until verification is complete;
- if your application is declined, payments you have accepted will be **refunded to the cardholders** and any fees charged on them will be waived.

Once the £2,000 limit is reached, further payments will be declined until verification is complete.

## 7. Ongoing due diligence

We are required to keep our information about you up to date. We will:

- ask you to reconfirm your business details at least every **24 months**;
- carry out additional checks if your processing patterns change significantly.

You must tell us within **14 days** of any change to your directors, PSCs, partners, legal entity, trading address or bank account, and at least **14 days in advance** of any change to the goods or services you sell (see MBFS-POL-10).

Changing your legal entity (for example, from sole trader to limited company) requires a **new application**; accounts cannot be transferred between legal entities.

## 8. Declined applications

- We may decline an application without giving detailed reasons, particularly where legal or regulatory restrictions prevent us from doing so.
- You may reapply after **6 months**, or sooner if the circumstances that led to the decision have changed (for example, you have obtained a required licence).
- If you think we have made a mistake, you can make a complaint under the *Complaints Handling Policy* (MBFS-POL-13).
