# Payment Terminal Hardware Policy

## 1. Terminal range

| | **BridgePay Mini** | **BridgePay Go** | **BridgePay Counter** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Market stalls, mobile traders, low volumes | Restaurants, tradespeople, table service | Shops and fixed tills |
| Connectivity | Bluetooth to the MerchantBridge app on your phone | 4G (data SIM included) and Wi-Fi | Ethernet and Wi-Fi |
| Receipt printer | No (digital receipts by SMS/email) | Yes | Yes |
| How you get it | **Buy** for **£59 + VAT** | **Rent** for **£15 + VAT per month** | **Rent** for **£12 + VAT per month** |
| Minimum term | None (you own it) | **12 months** | **12 months** |
| Warranty | **12 months** from delivery | For the life of the rental | For the life of the rental |
| Offline mode | No | Yes | Yes |

Terminals accept chip & PIN, contactless (cards, phones and wearables) and magnetic stripe.

## 2. Ordering and delivery

- Terminals can be ordered in the dashboard once your application has been submitted. Up to **10 terminals** can be ordered online; larger orders are handled by our sales team.
- **Standard delivery** is free and takes **3–5 business days**.
- **Next-business-day delivery** costs **£9.99 + VAT** per order if ordered before **15:00 UK time** on a business day.
- Your first order of a BridgePay Go or Counter includes **20 free paper rolls**. Additional rolls (57 mm × 40 mm thermal) cost **£12 + VAT per pack of 20**.

## 3. Rental terms

- Rental is charged monthly in advance on the 1st of each month, pro-rated for the first month.
- Each rented terminal has its own **12-month minimum term** starting on the date it is delivered. After 12 months the rental continues on a rolling monthly basis and can be ended with **30 days' notice**.
- Terminal rental agreements are **separate from your plan**. Cancelling or changing your plan does not end a terminal rental, and vice versa.
- **Early termination:** if you end a rental during the 12-month minimum term, an early termination fee equal to **50% of the remaining monthly rental** for the rest of the minimum term is payable, plus VAT. Termination takes effect at the end of the billing month in which we receive notice; "remaining" means the full billing months of the minimum term after that date.

## 4. Returning rented terminals

- When a rental ends you must return the terminal, with its charger and base, within **30 days** using the prepaid returns label we provide.
- If a terminal is not returned within 30 days, an **unreturned terminal fee** is charged: **£150 + VAT** (Go) or **£120 + VAT** (Counter).
- Purchased BridgePay Mini terminals can be returned **unused and in original packaging within 14 days** of delivery for a full refund.

## 5. Faults and warranty

- If a terminal develops a fault, first try the troubleshooting steps in the *Terminals and Hardware FAQ*.
- If the fault continues, call support. If a terminal is faulty and covered by warranty (or rented), we send a **replacement free of charge** by next-business-day delivery.
- The faulty terminal must be returned within **14 days** of receiving the replacement, or the replacement charge in section 6 applies.
- Warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or damage caused by unauthorised repairs.

## 6. Lost, stolen or damaged terminals

- Report a lost or stolen terminal **immediately** on our **24/7 emergency line, 020 7946 0999**, or in the dashboard. We will deactivate it remotely so it cannot be used to process refunds.
- Replacement charges for lost, stolen or non-warranty damaged terminals are:

| Terminal | Replacement charge |
|---|---|
| BridgePay Mini | £59 + VAT |
| BridgePay Go | £120 + VAT |
| BridgePay Counter | £95 + VAT |

- For rented terminals, **rental continues** on the replacement terminal under the original rental agreement; the minimum term does not restart.
- If a terminal was stolen, we recommend you report it to the police; a crime reference number is not required for a replacement.

## 7. Offline mode (Go and Counter)

If the terminal loses its connection, it can accept payments in offline mode:

- up to **50** offline transactions, each up to **£100**;
- offline transactions must be uploaded within **24 hours** by reconnecting the terminal;
- offline transactions are authorised **only when uploaded**. If a card is declined at that point, **you bear the loss** — MerchantBridge does not guarantee offline payments.

## 8. Contactless limits

MerchantBridge does not set a contactless limit. Limits are set by card issuers and most UK cards currently have a **£100** contactless limit per transaction. Above the card's limit, or periodically for security, the customer will be asked to insert their card and enter their PIN.

## 9. Software updates

Terminals update automatically overnight between **02:00 and 04:00 UK time**. Leave terminals switched on and charging overnight where possible. A terminal that has missed updates for more than 30 days may stop accepting payments until updated.
