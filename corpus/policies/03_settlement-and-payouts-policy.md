# Settlement and Payouts Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when and how money from your card sales is paid into your bank account. Changes to payout arrangements are announced in dated *Service Notices*, which take precedence over this policy from their effective date.

## 2. Key definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Business day** | Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England and Wales. |
| **Batch cut-off** | **22:00 UK time** on each business day. |
| **Processing day** | The business day on which a transaction's batch closes. Transactions captured **before 22:00 on a business day** belong to that day's batch. Transactions captured **after 22:00**, or on a **weekend or bank holiday**, belong to the batch of the **next business day**. |
| **Payout day** | The business day on which we send your money. |
| **Net settlement** | Your gross sales for a batch, minus transaction fees, refunds, chargebacks, reserves and any other amounts owed. |

## 3. Standard payout schedule

| Plan | Payout day |
|---|---|
| Starter | **2 business days** after the processing day (T+2) |
| Growth | **Next business day** after the processing day (T+1) |
| Scale | **Next business day** after the processing day (T+1) |
| Enterprise | As agreed in contract (same-day settlement available) |

Payouts are sent by **Faster Payments**, initiated at **09:00 UK time** on the payout day. Funds usually arrive within 2 hours, but your bank may take until the end of the payout day to credit them.

### Worked examples (no bank holidays)

- **Growth**, sale on **Tuesday at 14:00** → processing day Tuesday → paid **Wednesday**.
- **Growth**, sale on **Tuesday at 22:30** → processing day Wednesday → paid **Thursday**.
- **Growth**, sale on **Saturday** → processing day Monday → paid **Tuesday**.
- **Starter**, sale on **Friday at 10:00** → processing day Friday → paid **Tuesday** (Monday is day 1, Tuesday is day 2).
- **Starter**, sale on **Sunday** → processing day Monday → paid **Wednesday**.

Bank holidays move processing and payout days to the next business day. We publish a payout calendar ahead of Christmas, New Year and Easter as a Service Notice.

## 4. Bank account requirements

- Payouts in GBP are made to a **UK bank account** held in the name of the business (or the sole trader's own name).
- Payouts in EUR or USD (Growth and Scale only) are covered in MBFS-POL-08.
- To change your bank account you must use the dashboard with two-factor authentication. For your protection, the **first payout to a new bank account is delayed by 72 hours**. We will never ask you to change bank details by phone or email (see MBFS-POL-09).

## 5. Minimum payout and carried-forward balances

- The minimum payout is **£1** (or €1 / $1). Balances below this are carried forward to the next payout day.
- If your balance is **negative** (for example, because refunds or chargebacks exceed sales), we will recover the shortfall from future settlements or by Direct Debit.

## 6. Returned payouts

If a payout is returned by your bank (for example, because the account is closed or the details are wrong), we charge a **returned payout fee of £5** and hold the funds until you provide valid bank details.

## 7. Payout holds and reserves

We may delay or withhold payouts where required to manage risk or to meet legal obligations. Payout holds, rolling reserves and closing reserves are explained in the *Rolling Reserves and Risk Holds Policy* (MBFS-POL-11). Payouts are not made until account verification is complete (MBFS-POL-01).

## 8. Instant Payouts

Instant Payouts let you send your available balance to your bank account at any time, including weekends and bank holidays, instead of waiting for your standard payout.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | **Growth and Scale plans only** |
| **Fee** | **1.0% of the payout amount**, minimum **£0.50** per Instant Payout (VAT-exempt) |
| **Limits** | Up to **£10,000** per Instant Payout and up to **5** Instant Payouts per day |
| **Eligibility** | Account verified and open for at least **30 days**, no active payout hold or reserve review |
| **Speed** | Usually within **30 minutes**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week |
| **Currency** | GBP only |

Only **available balance** can be paid out instantly — funds from transactions in a batch that has not yet closed are not available.

## 9. Missing payouts

If a payout has not arrived by the end of the payout day:

1. check the payout status and bank details in your dashboard;
2. check for any notification of a hold or reserve;
3. contact support quoting the payout reference. We will trace the payment within **2 business days**.
