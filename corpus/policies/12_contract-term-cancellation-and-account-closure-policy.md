# Contract Term, Cancellation and Account Closure Policy

## 1. Contract terms by plan

| Plan | Term | How to end it |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Rolling, no minimum term | Cancel any time; closure takes effect immediately |
| Growth | Rolling monthly, no minimum term | **30 days' notice** |
| Scale | **12-month initial term** from the date the plan starts, then rolling monthly | During the initial term: early termination fee applies (section 3). After the initial term: **30 days' notice** |
| Enterprise | As set out in your contract | As set out in your contract |

**Terminal rentals** are separate agreements with their own 12-month minimum term (MBFS-POL-06). **BridgeAdvance** agreements are also separate (MBFS-POL-15).

## 2. How to cancel

- The account Owner can cancel in the dashboard (Settings → Account → Close account), or by emailing **support@merchantbridge.example** from the email address registered to the Owner.
- We will confirm cancellation in writing and tell you the date it takes effect, any fees due and the closing reserve period.
- Cancelling your account automatically gives notice on all terminal rentals linked to it.

## 3. Early termination fees

**Scale plan during the initial 12-month term**

- Termination takes effect at the **end of the billing month** in which we receive your notice.
- An early termination fee (ETF) of **50% of the monthly plan fees remaining** in the initial term is payable. "Remaining" means the full billing months of the initial term **after** the termination date.
- The ETF is calculated on the VAT-exclusive plan fee (£79) and **VAT at 20%** is added.

> *Example:* a Scale plan starts on **1 June 2026**, so the initial term covers June 2026 to May 2027. Notice is received on **11 November 2026**, so termination takes effect on **30 November 2026**. Remaining billing months: December to May = **6**. ETF = 6 × £79 × 50% = **£237.00 + VAT (£47.40) = £284.40**. The November plan fee, already billed, is not refunded.

**Terminal rentals during the 12-month minimum term** — 50% of the remaining monthly rental, calculated the same way, plus VAT (MBFS-POL-06).

**No ETF is payable** if:

- you terminate before a price increase takes effect, following a notice of that increase (MBFS-POL-02, section 10);
- MerchantBridge closes your account for reasons other than your breach of the agreement;
- you are moving to the Enterprise plan with MerchantBridge.

## 4. What happens when your account closes

1. You can no longer take new payments from the termination date.
2. Monthly fees stop from the termination date. Fees already billed for the current month are not refunded.
3. Any outstanding fees, early termination fees, negative balances and outstanding **BridgeAdvance** balances become due immediately and are deducted from your balance.
4. Remaining funds may be held as a **closing reserve** for up to **120 days** (or 180 days for advance-payment businesses) to cover late refunds and chargebacks (MBFS-POL-11). The balance is then paid out.
5. Rented terminals must be returned within **30 days** (MBFS-POL-06).
6. You can download statements and reports from the dashboard for **12 months** after closure. Your data is retained as described in MBFS-POL-14.

## 5. Closure by MerchantBridge

- We may close your account by giving you at least **two months' notice**.
- We may suspend or close your account **immediately** where: we suspect fraud or money laundering; you breach the agreement in a serious way (including selling prohibited goods); your chargeback ratio is persistently above 1.50%; you become insolvent; or a card scheme, regulator or the law requires it.
- Accounts with **no transactions for 18 months** may be closed with two months' notice.

## 6. Reopening an account

Closed accounts cannot be reopened. You can apply for a new account; a new application is subject to full verification (MBFS-POL-01).
